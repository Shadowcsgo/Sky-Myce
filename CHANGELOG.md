# Sky-Myce changelog

Newest first. Each release adds a `## <version> (<date>)` section with one line per player-facing change. The mod shows
this file in the Daily Digest, the update prompt shows the section for the new version, and Discord gets a copy.

## 2.8.0-beta-047 (2026-11-02)
- Custom names wrap properly in chat and in the NPC dialogue box instead of running past the edge, and no longer show twice in the box.
- The menu is much more compact: slimmer sidebar, smaller Home tiles, features as one-line rows with their on/off switch, and settings as divided rows inside one panel per feature.
- Settings rows: the control sits beside the name, Pin is an icon, and Reset only appears on settings you changed.
- Presets: Best performance, Balanced and Best looks (graphics, animations, chat media), and NPC lines in the box or in chat. Find them on the Settings page or by searching.
- Menu search also finds presets and commands; a command result puts it in the chat box for you.
- What's New: Home shows this version's changes, and the Daily Digest has the full changelog.

## 2.8.0-beta-046 (2026-11-02)
- Commission Highlight: highlights the blocks your open commissions need and outlines slayer mobs, including in Mineshafts.
- Spread only counts blocks that really broke while you were mining them, only with a pickaxe or drill, never in the Catacombs, and animates its procs.
- Pickobulus scans nearby veins and points you to the best one.

## 2.8.0-beta-045 (2026-11-02)
- /sm graphics reports FPS, p95 frame time, the menu's own CPU cost and whether background blur is on.

## 2.8.0-beta-044 (2026-11-02)
- Greenhouse workspace with Overview, Mutations, Shopping, Unlock guide and History tabs.
- Click a greenhouse grid cell to lock it; Plan best and Remove (with confirmation) on plot cards.

## 2.8.0-beta-043 (2026-11-02)
- Settings show Basic options first; Advanced and Expert options are collapsed until you open them or search.
- Every setting has a Reset button while it differs from its default.

## 2.8.0-beta-042 (2026-11-02)
- New Animations setting, independent of the graphics mode.
- Home shows search, your tools, favorites and recent features.

## 2.8.0-beta-041 (2026-11-02)
- Text stays sharp at every graphics mode, and menu repaints are about 4.5x cheaper.
- Six categories (Dungeons, Gameplay, Economy, Community, Appearance, Settings) with hover submenus.
- Searchable /sm help.

## 2.8.0-beta-040 (2026-11-01)
- Greenhouse: Thunderling stops at 16,000 charge (stage 8) until discharged; reminders show when.
