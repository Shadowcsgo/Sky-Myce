# Dungeons
Find a party, route through the floor, coordinate bosses, and track your runs and rewards.

**Parties and Party Tools**
- **Party Finder**: See which dungeon friends are online and who is looking for a group, send invites and filter parties.
- **Spirit Leap Menu**: Pick who to leap to from a menu with four gameplay previews, rendered on your own client.

**Routes and Navigation**
- **Dungeon Routing**: Shows the way through the doors to Blood, and can take you past starred mobs on the way if you want.

**Run Tracking and Splits**
- **Dungeon Tracker**: Your run history, what you looted, and an estimate of what each run was worth.
- **Dungeon Splits**: Live splits while you run, plus your personal bests, best possible time and averages for each floor.
- **Dungeon Win Screen**: After a completed run, a summary overlay shows how it went.

**Boss Coordination**
- **Goldor Coordination**: A map of the F7/M7 terminals, routes for each ADG role, stacks and timed pre-leaps, shared with your party in party chat.
- **Crush Camera**: A fixed camera view for your class, from 26 seconds into Storm until Storm dies.

**Rewards and Dungeon Information**
- **Dungeon Chests**: Estimated profit for each chest, which loot is valuable, and protection against buying or rerolling by mistake.
- **Dungeon Chat Filter**: Pick which dungeon messages stay in your chat.

# Gameplay
Combat and abilities, mining, inventory helpers, instances and information.

**Combat and Abilities**
- **Ability Cooldowns**: Cooldown timers for the ability on the item you're holding, and a heads-up when it's ready.
- **Loadout Keybinds**: Swap between equipment loadouts with keys you choose, with safeguards for menus.
- **Bal Notifications**: Tells your party when Bal spawns and when its health reaches milestones.

**Instances and Requeue**
- **Instance Helpers**: Alerts when someone is missing from your dungeon or Kuudra party, and handles requeuing for both.

**Mining**
- **Mining Helpers**: Highlights your commission targets, follows up on Mining Spread, finds the best vein for Pickobulus and points the way when the spot is off screen, can draw gemstone glass and panes in wool so they are easier to spot, and shows Crystal Hollows lobby info.

**Inventory and Interactions**
- **Auto Refill**: Pick which consumables get topped up when you run out.
- **NPC Dialogue**: NPC lines appear in a dialogue box with the speaker's portrait, and you pick your answer with the number keys.
- **Sign Enter**: Press Enter to finish typing in Auction House, Bazaar and other signs.

**Information**
- **Information Overlays**: Shows client details, your skill XP progress and your estimated HOTM and Skill XP per hour on screen.

# Economy
Auctions, the Garden, item values and what your stash is worth.

**Auctions and Bazaar**
- **Auction Browser**: Search cached auction listings, with filters, bids and item lore.

**Item Values**
- **Price History**: Hover an item in any menu and press P to see its Bazaar or auction price graph.

**Garden and Greenhouse**
- **Greenhouse Planner**: Shows your greenhouses, ranks mutations by profit for each plot, and gives you layouts, growth timers, harvest profit and ghost blocks. Type /sm greenhouse help for details.

**Stash and Inventory Value**
- **Stash Value Filter**: Filters low-value items out of your stash and stops you picking up what you don't want.

# Community
IRC chat, friends, leaderboards, the RNG Journal and the Daily Digest.

**Leaderboards**
- **Community Leaderboards**: Compare yourself with other players who opted in. Every entry says where its data came from, and periods are in UTC.

**RNG Journal and Progress**
- **RNG Journal**: Search your own saved drops and acquisitions, with dates, counts and the history the mod measured.

**Daily Digest and Updates**
- **Daily Digest**: A daily look at your progress and public updates, plus community RNG reports if you turn them on.

**IRC and Chat**
- **IRC Chat**: Global chat shared between Minecraft and Discord, and a Party IRC that stays Minecraft only. Open it with /sm irc or /sm party.
- **Party Commands**: Choose which party commands are on, set their cooldowns, and optionally fall back to the stats API.

**Friends and Players**
- **Player Utilities**: Cached friend activity, where they are, and profile information.

# Appearance
Player cosmetics, HUD and widgets, themes and colors.

**HUD and Widgets**
- **Status Bars**: Separate animated bars for health, mana, defense, speed, vitality, respiration and experience that you can arrange around the hotbar.
- **HUD**: Arrange your widgets, choose which are visible and set their scale.

**Player Cosmetics**
- **Player Cosmetics**: Pick cosmetics, and control how names are shown and how players are scaled.

**Themes and Colors**
- **Appearance**: Pick a theme preset: the palette and component style for every menu and HUD widget.

**Animations**
- **Animations**: Choose how much the menus move, from Expressive down to Off. Reduced keeps the hover and press feedback.

# Settings
Controls, performance and graphics, privacy and account.

**Privacy and Account**
- **Discord & Community**: Connect your Discord, choose how your community data is shared (On, On (anon) or Off), and updates.

**Performance and Graphics**
- **Performance & Graphics**: Graphics mode controls shadows, glow, background blur and GUI animations. Auto adjusts to hold 60 FPS, and text stays sharp in every mode.

**Controls and Keybinds**
- **Controls**: Choose the key that opens the Sky-Myce menu.

# Commands
Everything here is also in the /sm menu. These are just shortcuts.
- `/sm`: Opens the Sky-Myce menu with search, categories and your tools
- `/sm settings`: Opens Settings
- `/sm hud`: Arrange your HUD widgets
- `/sm bars`: Arrange your status bars
- `/sm pf`: Party Finder: online dungeon friends and LFG
- `/sm dungeon`: Dungeon Tracker: your runs, loot and rewards
- `/sm splits`: Dungeon Splits: live splits and personal bests
- `/sm m7pb [player]`: Look up someone's M7 personal best
- `/sm ah`: Opens the Auction Browser
- `/sm greenhouse`: Greenhouse profit ranking; /sm greenhouse gui opens the planner
- `/sm journal`: Opens the RNG Journal
- `/sm digest`: Opens the Daily Digest
- `/sm friends`: Friends and player activity
- `/sm leaderboard`: Community leaderboards
- `/sm cosmetics`: Player cosmetics
- `/sm irc [message]`: Global Minecraft/Discord chat (alias /sm global); add a message to send it once. In chat, click ↩ to reply, + to react, ⋯ to edit
- `/sm party [message]`: Minecraft-only Party IRC (alias /sm pc)
- `/sm msg <name> <message>`: Send a direct message; /sm reply (or /sm r) answers the last one
